# Copy decomposition

## Goal

Convert raw promotional copy into a small number of visual reading units. Do not rewrite the commercial claim unless requested.

## Extract these fields

1. Context label: city, industry, group, or identity.
2. Primary hook: the number, contrast, aspiration, or pain point that stops the scroll.
3. Proof or comparison: a second number, a real scene, or a concise contrast.
4. Decision line: the takeaway the viewer should accept.
5. CTA: the requested action and exact reply keyword.

## Compression rules

- Keep the full on-screen copy under roughly 55 Chinese characters when possible.
- Keep a display line around 8-13 Chinese characters.
- Put the strongest number or contrast in the largest type.
- Remove spoken filler before removing proof.
- If the copy remains too dense, move supporting detail to the post caption rather than shrinking all type.
- Preserve exact percentages, years, amounts, city names, and CTA keywords supplied by the user.

## Example

Input:

> 大健康行业的私域复购率是40%，美妆只有15%。选赛道这件事，数据不会骗人。现在还想进军这个赛道的，PL区回复：勾兑

Display units:

- Context label: `大健康行业`
- Primary hook: `私域复购率 40%`
- Comparison: `美妆只有 15%`
- Decision: `选赛道这件事 / 数据不会骗人`
- CTA setup: `现在还想进军这个赛道的`
- CTA: `PL区回复：勾兑`

The percentage statement remains the user's claim. State in delivery that it was not independently verified unless a source check was requested and completed.
